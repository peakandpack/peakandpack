PEAK AND PACK WEBSITE: SETUP GUIDE (for beginners, no experience needed)

What you have: a folder of files that make up your website. GitHub Pages is a free service
that shows those files to the world as a website. You upload the files once, switch one
setting on, and your site is live. Total time: about 15 minutes.

WORDS YOU WILL SEE
- Repository ("repo"): a folder on GitHub that holds your website's files.
- Commit: GitHub's word for "save". Every time you change something you press "Commit changes".

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1. Unzip the files on your computer
------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click the zip you downloaded. You get a folder called "peakandpack-site" containing:
  index.html, style.css, app.js, content.js, favicon.png, SETUP-GUIDE.txt, and two folders: assets and photos.
Test it first: double-click index.html. Your site opens in your browser.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2. Create a free GitHub account
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://github.com and click "Sign up".
2. Use peakandpack@gmail.com, choose a password and a username. The username becomes part of
   your site's web address, so pick something short, e.g. "peakandpack".
3. Confirm your email address when GitHub sends you a code.
4. Choose the FREE plan.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3. Create a repository
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Click the "+" at the top right, then "New repository".
2. Repository name: peakandpack-site
3. Choose "Public". (Free GitHub Pages needs a public repository. This is fine: your website
   is public anyway. Do not put anything private in these files.)
4. Tick "Add a README file".
5. Click "Create repository".

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4. Upload the website files
------------------------------------------------------------
1. In your new repository, click "Add file" then "Upload files".
2. Open the unzipped "peakandpack-site" folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING INSIDE it
   (all the files and the two folders "assets" and "photos"), and drag it into the browser window.
   Do not upload the zip itself, and do not drag in the outer folder: drag its contents.
3. Wait until all files show as uploaded.
4. Scroll down and click "Commit changes".
5. Check the repository now lists index.html, content.js, assets, photos and so on.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5. Switch the website on
------------------------------------------------------------
1. In your repository click "Settings" (top menu), then "Pages" (left menu).
2. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to "Deploy from a branch".
3. Under "Branch" choose "main" and the folder "/ (root)", then click Save.
4. Wait 1 to 3 minutes and refresh the Pages settings page. A box appears saying
   "Your site is live at ..."
   The address will be: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/peakandpack-site/
5. Open it. That is your website. Share that link.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6. Change courses, dates, articles
------------------------------------------------------------
Almost everything you will ever change lives in ONE file: content.js.
1. In your repository, click content.js, then the pencil icon (Edit this file).
2. Change the text. Keep the quote marks and the commas at the end of lines.
   - New date: copy an existing line in the "dates" list and change it. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.
   - Date is full: change full:false to full:true on its line. The site then shows "Full · waiting list".
   - Dates that have passed disappear on their own.
   - New article: copy the block in "articles" and edit it. Use \n\n for a blank line between paragraphs.
3. Click "Commit changes" (green button), then "Commit changes" again.
4. After about a minute, refresh your website. Use Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) if you
   still see the old version.
If you break something (a missing comma or quote mark makes the page go empty), open the file
again, click "History" at the top right, and restore the previous version. Nothing is ever lost.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7. Add photos
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Resize photos first so the site stays fast: about 1600 pixels wide, under 500 KB each.
2. In your repository open the "photos" folder, click "Add file", then "Upload files", drag the
   photos in, and commit. Use simple file names without spaces, e.g. lyngen-sunrise.jpg.
3. Open content.js, find the "photos" list, and add a line for each photo:
     { file: "lyngen-sunrise.jpg", caption: "Sunrise above Lyngenfjord" },
4. Commit. The photos appear on the Photos page.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8. Booking requests (works from day one, upgrade is optional)
------------------------------------------------------------
As delivered: when someone fills in the booking form and presses "Send request", their own
email app opens with the request already written, addressed to peakandpack@gmail.com. They
press send. This needs no setup. The downside is that it fails for people who do not use an
email app on their device.

Optional upgrade, so requests arrive in your inbox without the visitor's email app:
1. Go to https://formspree.io and create a free account with peakandpack@gmail.com.
2. Click "New form", name it "Bookings", and set the email to peakandpack@gmail.com.
3. Formspree shows a form address that looks like https://formspree.io/f/abcdwxyz. Copy it.
4. In content.js, paste it between the quotes:   formEndpoint: "https://formspree.io/f/abcdwxyz",
5. Commit. Then send yourself a test booking from the site. Formspree emails you to confirm
   the first submission.
Formspree's free plan has a monthly limit on submissions. Check their pricing page for the
current number. If you outgrow it, the email method keeps working as a fallback.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9. Your own web address (optional, not free)
------------------------------------------------------------
Something like peakandpack.no costs roughly 100 to 200 NOK a year from a domain seller.
Once you own it: Settings, Pages, "Custom domain", type it in, and follow GitHub's
instructions for pointing the domain at GitHub. Ask me and I will walk you through it.

------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
- Page shows "404": wait 3 minutes after step 5, then refresh. Check Settings, Pages, branch "main".
- Site looks unstyled: you probably uploaded the outer folder. The files must sit directly in the
  repository, not inside another folder.
- Logo missing: the "assets" folder was not uploaded. Upload it again.
- Change not showing: wait a minute, then hard-refresh (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R).
- Page is blank after an edit: a comma or quote mark in content.js is missing. Restore it via History.
